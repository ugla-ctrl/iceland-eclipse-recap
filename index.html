The Moment

At 17:47 UTC on 12 August 2026, totality swept across Snæfellsnes for 2 minutes and 7 seconds.

Iceland's last total solar eclipse was in 1954. The next won't happen until 2196. For one afternoon, the path of totality ran straight through Hellissandur, and totality overlapped the Perseid meteor shower, a pairing that won't come around again.

The Setting · Hellissandur

"Iceland in miniature."

Home to just 400 people and set at the westernmost edge of Iceland with minimal light pollution, Hellissandur put guests within reach of glaciers, black sand beaches, lava fields and ice caves, all inside one geologically dense peninsula.

Icelandic and international artists brought local stories, landscapes and legends to life across the town in the Murals of Hellissandur, a living, community-driven canvas that guests could walk between programming blocks.

The Partnership

IMXP + Secret Solstice

IMXP has built and grown the biggest events at the intersection of total solar eclipses, music and technology, producing Texas Eclipse Festival in 2024 before Iceland. Founded in 2014, Secret Solstice is one of Iceland's biggest and highest-rated international festivals: 18K attendees, 70+ global and Icelandic artists, 100% renewable geothermal power, ranked in the top 7% most digitally engaged festival audiences globally.
